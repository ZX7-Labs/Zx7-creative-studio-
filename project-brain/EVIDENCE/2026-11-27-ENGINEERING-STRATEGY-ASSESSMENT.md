## Architecture judgment

Keep Brain/BODY, source-owning native engines, one project envelope, host permissions, revision authority, custody, physical feedback, human co-edit and recovery. No scratch rewrite, universal file format, parallel registry/store/scheduler. Seek originality in machine-native creative perception, expressive composition and efficient control. The Brain chooses art, method, relevant semantic observations and revision; trusted code owns mechanical preconditions, execution, evidence and comparable verifier alignment. Technical receipts do not establish what the model saw/heard.

G2 matched native/procedural/adaptive comparison is an unproven hypothesis; reject additional abstraction if it does not earn quality/latency/cost/fidelity benefits. Preserve full A–L even if a route fails.

## Founder and engineering behavior

Maximum quality is a direction, not unconstrained simultaneous maximization of conflicting metrics. Define hard correctness, security, rights floors and workload-specific tradeoffs. Full scope need not mean every domain simultaneously or mandatory video-first. Select by dependency/information value. Continuous activity and CI counts are not outcome progress. Engineering assistants also share responsibility for prioritizing low-risk repairs over hardest uncertainty.

**Decision:** preserve product vision and sound foundations; change unit of progress to reproducible accepted capability or causally resolved uncertainty. Stabilize canonical baseline, isolate blockers, prove genuine creative loop, measure alternatives, close full A–L and release gates. No automatic launch, private egress, paid expansion or destructive work authorized.
